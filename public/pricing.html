Frequently Asked Questions
  • help_outlineCan I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel your subscription at any time from your Settings page. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

  • help_outlineWhat counts as a "dream analysis"?

    Each time you submit a dream description and receive an AI-generated Jungian analysis, that counts as one analysis. Viewing your existing analyses doesn't count.

  • help_outlineIs my dream data private?

    Your dreams are stored securely in Firebase and are only accessible by your account. We do not share your data with third parties.

  • help_outlineWhat AI model powers the analysis?

    DreamCoach uses Google's Gemini 1.5 Pro, one of the most advanced AI models available, trained on extensive psychological and dream analysis literature.
