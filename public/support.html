The Pioneers of Dream Analysis

Discover the foundational frameworks our AI uses to map the landscapes of your subconscious mind.

Carl Jung

Analytical Psychology

Jung believed dreams are the window to the "collective unconscious"—a shared psychological heritage. His method focuses on universally recognized symbols, called archetypes (like The Shadow, The Hero, or The Anima). Jungian analysis treats dreams as forward-looking tools designed to help us achieve "individuation," the journey of becoming our most authentic selves.

Sigmund Freud

Psychoanalysis

Dubbed the "royal road to the unconscious," Freud saw dreams as expressions of deeply repressed wishes, desires, and unresolved childhood conflicts. His method separates the "manifest content" (what literally happens in the dream) from the "latent content" (the hidden, symbolic truth). Freudian analysis aims to dig up the past to resolve current emotional knots.

Getting Started

How do I record my first dream? expand_more

Sign in to DreamCoach and you'll see the dream entry form on the home page. Fill in the date (defaults to today), an optional name, and a description of your dream — aim for at least 20 characters. Click Analyze Dream and our AI will generate a full Jungian analysis within seconds.

Tip: record your dream immediately after waking up while the details are fresh!

What does the free plan include? expand_more

The free plan gives you 3 dream analyses per month, access to your full dream journal history on the Dashboard, and Jungian AI analysis. You can upgrade to Pro for unlimited analyses, pattern trend analysis, and export features.

How do I use the Pattern Analysis feature? expand_more

Pattern Analysis is a Pro feature. Go to the Analysis page, select a date range, choose or type a prompt (e.g. "What recurring themes appear in my dreams?"), and click Run Analysis. The AI will analyze up to 50 dreams from that period and return thematic insights.

How do I set up a daily dream reminder? expand_more

On the Dashboard, find the "Set a Dream Reminder" card. Choose your preferred time and click Daily or Weekly — this opens Google Calendar with a pre-filled recurring reminder event ready for you to save.

Account

How do I change my display name? expand_more

Go to Settings → Profile, update your display name, and click Save Profile.

How do I delete my account? expand_more

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Type "DELETE" in the confirmation field and confirm. All your dreams and personal data will be permanently removed. This action cannot be undone.

Is my dream data private? expand_more

Yes. Your dream journal is protected by Firebase Security Rules — only you can access your entries. We never sell or share your dream data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Billing & Subscription

How do I upgrade to Pro? expand_more

Go to the Pricing page, choose Monthly ($20/mo) or Yearly ($192/yr), and complete checkout via PayPal. Your account is upgraded instantly — no waiting required.

Do you have a coupon or promo code? expand_more

Yes! If you have a coupon code, enter it in the "Have a Coupon?" section on the Pricing page to unlock free Pro access.

How do I cancel my subscription? expand_more

Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No penalties or hidden fees.

What is the 30-Day Moonphase Guarantee? expand_more

If you don't gain meaningful insight within your first 30 days on a Pro plan, contact us at service@dream-analytics.com and we'll issue a full refund. No questions asked.
