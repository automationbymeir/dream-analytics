Psychoanalysis

Sigmund Freud

The father of psychoanalysis who paved the royal road to the unconscious.

psychology

The Core Philosophy

Freud fundamentally changed psychology by viewing the unconscious as a vast reservoir of unacceptable thoughts, wishes, and feelings. He believed that everything we hide from our waking selves bubbles to the surface while we sleep.

flare

Manifest vs. Latent Content

Freud theorized that the bizarre elements of a dream (Manifest content) act as a dense disguise for our deep, repressed, and often taboo desires (Latent content). Dreams must wrap our desires in strange visual metaphors so we don't wake up distressed.

explore

Wish Fulfillment

According to Freud, at its absolute core, every dream is an attempt by our subconscious to satisfy an instinctual urge or wish that society, morality, or our waking ego strictly forbids and represses.
